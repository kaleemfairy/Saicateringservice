Our story

Rooted in Vellore, ready for Chennai.

What started as a family kitchen serving neighborhood weddings has grown into a full-service catering team trusted across a 30–40 km radius of Vellore. Every menu is built on traditional Tamil recipes, cooked fresh on-site, and served the way a home kitchen would serve family.

We're now taking bookings in Chennai — bringing the same standard of freshness and hospitality to the city, for weddings, receptions, corporate events, and private functions.
